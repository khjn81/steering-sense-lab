The SDK's [original privacy notice](dist/vendor/mediapipe/README.md#privacy-notice) states that input processing is on device and that API metrics may be sent to Google. This application server restricts successful network connections to its own origin through CSP. The project does not upload camera frames or store recordings; this does not replace the SDK notice or a future hosting provider's policy.

[Asset hashes](quality/assets.json), [upstream verification](quality/upstream-verification.json), and [original dependency sources/integrity](dependencies.json) identify the distributed bytes.
